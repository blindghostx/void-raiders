Progress and iCloud

My progress didn't move to my new device.

Turn on Settings ▸ iCloud ▸ Sync Progress & Stats in the game on both devices, sign in to the same Apple Account, then open Level Select once on each.

How do I start over?

Settings ▸ Progress ▸ Reset Progress re-locks the levels. Settings ▸ Stats ▸ Reset Stats clears scores and stars. With iCloud sync on, a reset applies on all your devices.

Game Center

Scores aren't showing on the leaderboards.

Check that you're signed in to Game Center (iOS Settings ▸ Game Center) and that Settings ▸ Game Center ▸ Leaderboards & Achievements is on in the game.

Co-op

Co-op can't find my friend.

Both devices need Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on and should be close together. If you tapped "Don't Allow" on the local network prompt, turn it back on in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Local Network ▸ Void Raiders.

It says my friend is running a different version.

Both devices need the same version of Void Raiders. Update both from the App Store, then try again.

Controls and performance

Can I use a controller?

Yes: MFi, Xbox and PlayStation controllers. Left stick moves, B / Circle dashes, Menu pauses. A keyboard and mouse work on iPad and Mac.

The game feels hot or slow.

Try Settings ▸ Graphics ▸ Effects Quality ▸ Low, or turn on Settings ▸ Power ▸ Battery Saver. It also turns on by itself in iOS Low Power Mode.
