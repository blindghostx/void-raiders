Void Raiders — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026 · Vira Technologies LLC

Void Raiders is a game. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't collect, store, sell or share your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your settings, progress, stars, best scores and stats are saved on your device. If you add the home-screen widget, it reads a small summary of those numbers (for example your Daily Run score) from shared storage on the same device.

Apple services you can choose to use

  • Game Center. If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Apple's Game Center, where your Game Center name can appear on leaderboards. You can turn this off in the game (Settings ▸ Game Center) or in the iOS Settings app.
  • iCloud. If iCloud sync is on (Settings ▸ iCloud in the game), your progress and stats are kept in your own iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices. We can't see that data.

These services are run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Playing with a friend nearby

Local co-op connects two nearby devices directly over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth using Apple's Multipeer Connectivity. While you host or look for a game, the name in the co-op lobby's YOUR NAME field is visible to nearby devices looking for a Void Raiders game. That name starts as your device's name (usually just "iPhone" or "iPad") and is saved only on your device. Once connected, the two devices exchange gameplay data such as positions and scores only with each other. iOS asks for your permission to use the local network first. Nothing goes to us.

Sharing

When you tap SHARE RESULT, the game creates an image and a line of text and opens the standard iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes, if anywhere.

Children

Void Raiders collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
